- The EQ has up to 24 bands: a double-click on its display makes a band there, delete frees its
  slot without renumbering the others, and each band is placed Stereo, Left, Right, Mid or Side
  with its own curve colour, or split into a left/right or mid/side pair. A tilt shelf and a flat
  tilt join the band types. A band can be Dynamic (its gain moves with its level) or Spectral
  (only the narrow parts that stand out move), with its range, threshold, attack, release and
  detail, and a live curve shows what moves. The panel is a band column beside a larger display
  with an output meter: values can be typed (a frequency also as a note or as 6k3), several bands
  selected and dragged together, a band held to hear it alone, a curve drawn to make bands, and
  the analyser set. Its expanded panel is a strip over the device row, with the selected band's
  controls floating under the curve once the strip is tall; while it is open the compact panel
  lists the bands. Projects saved with the old two curves load with the same sound.
