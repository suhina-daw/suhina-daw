- The NAM Amp Modeler and NAM Cab Loader have stock presets under *Stock presets* in the browser,
  five each, that set their controls (the amp's gain, EQ, HQ, delay and volume; the cabinet's
  phase, high pass, cuts, delay and volume) and leave the model or impulse response the device
  runs as the user has set it. `device.add {stock}` and `track.add {stock}` add one by its key,
  dragging one from the browser does the same, and each is one undo step.
