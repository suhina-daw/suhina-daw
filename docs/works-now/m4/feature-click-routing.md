- The metronome's click plays pre-fader by default: devices, fader and mute on the main channel
  leave it alone. Its right-click menu, in the control bar and the main strip, can put it
  post-fader, on the cue output only or through the main channel, and picks its sound by family
  (Tones, and Soft with its four variations) and a sound of its own for a bar's first beat; each
  folder of sound files in the library's `Sounds/Metronome` is a family of its own. The click
  stays in time with a main channel that adds latency.
