- Instrument's modal source can play experimental **bar and tine bodies**: Marimba, Vibraphone,
  Xylophone and Glockenspiel bars and a plucked Kalimba tine. Bars have per-note tuned modes with
  wood or metal decay, a tube resonator under the marimba, xylophone and vibraphone bars, strike
  position on free-free bar mode shapes, and a mallet whose hardness, weight and velocity set the
  contact time and the knock of its head, so harder and faster strokes sound brighter. The tine has
  clamped-free modes, starts from a pluck and rings into a small box resonance. Tuning, Ring and
  Torsion shape the chosen body, and the Model page shows the body as a drawing whose strike point
  follows Strike. A key is one bar, so a roll strikes the same ringing bar again instead of
  stacking voices; bars spread across the stereo image by key. The vibraphone motor and damper
  pedal, and calibration against recordings, are not done yet.
