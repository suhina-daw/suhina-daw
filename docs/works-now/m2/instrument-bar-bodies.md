- Instrument's modal source can play two experimental **bar bodies**, Marimba bar and Vibraphone
  bar: per-note tuned bar modes with wood or aluminium decay, strike position on free-free bar
  mode shapes, and a mallet whose hardness and velocity set the contact time, so harder and
  faster strokes sound brighter. A key is one bar, so a roll strikes the same ringing bar again
  instead of stacking voices. Resonators, the vibraphone motor and damper pedal, and calibration
  are not done yet.
