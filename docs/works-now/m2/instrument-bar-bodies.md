- Instrument's modal source can play two experimental **bar bodies**, Marimba bar and Vibraphone
  bar: per-note tuned bar modes with wood or aluminium decay, a tube resonator under each bar, strike
  position on free-free bar mode shapes, and a mallet whose hardness and velocity set the contact
  time and the knock of its head, so harder and faster strokes sound brighter. A key is one bar, so
  a roll strikes the same ringing bar again instead of stacking voices; bars spread across the
  stereo image by key. The vibraphone motor and damper pedal, and calibration, are not done yet.
