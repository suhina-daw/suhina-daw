- The time signature is changed in the window: a click on the control bar's signature opens a
  field for any signature (`6/8`, `15/16`) and the common ones, for the song's start signature
  (`transport.setTimeSignature`) or the change at the playhead; the Arrangement ruler's
  right-click menu sets the signature from the nearest bar line or removes a change there, and a
  change's line on the ruler drags to another bar
  ([spec](docs/spec/tempo-map.md#editing-in-the-window)).
