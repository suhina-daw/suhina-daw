- Instrument fits held sounds too: a bowed, blown or sung sample (cello, violin, flute, voice,
  organ) keeps its vibrato (measured, then removed before fitting so its partials are found), the
  swell of each partial, partial levels that follow the vibrato, a band noise layer for bow or
  breath noise while the key is held, a measured release, and a spectral envelope that stays in
  Hz so notes far from the sample's key do not sound like a smaller instrument. The fit report
  names the vibrato, noise and release it found. It reproduces measured statistics of one
  recording, not a physical bowing or breath model.
