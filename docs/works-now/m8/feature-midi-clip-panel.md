- MIDI clip panel (#1222, #1214): a selected MIDI clip's column in the detail area is the same compact panel as an audio clip's (its colour, name, length and the Clip / Devices switch; Start and End, Loop, Position and Length with Set to the playhead, and its scale), with the note tools beside it on two tabs: Notes (Transpose, Fit to scale to the clip's scale, Invert, Reverse, Legato, ×2 and ÷2, and what is selected in figures: notes, pitch, span, velocity range) and Transform (Quantise, Humanise, Randomise velocity and Ramp velocity, each with its settings and an Apply button that says what it acts on). Humanise moves notes by milliseconds, mostly a little and never past its limit, keeps chords together and ends in place; `note.humanize` takes `ms` and `chords`. With the detail area stacked the tools go under the Clip part.
