- Instrument can fit an experimental model from an isolated decaying or held sample: up to 64
  resonances with their phases, fast-then-slow decays and beating partners, plus the attack the
  resonances do not explain, at the sample's own level. Accept it as one undoable edit, then
  **Add to model** recordings of other notes, velocities or instruments: notes in between fuse
  the recordings' resonances in parameter space. **Fit folder…** fits a folder of one-note
  recordings at once, files of one note as velocity layers. **Play original** and **Play
  fitted** compare the fit with its source at one level, with a spectral distance. The model
  stays editable with the body
  controls and reopens without fitting; the source sample is retained. Room and microphone are
  not separated, and calibrated realism across notes and dynamics is not established.
